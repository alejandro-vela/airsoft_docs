Delete Your Account

AirsoftOps  ·  Account & Data Deletion Request

You can request the permanent deletion of your AirsoftOps account and all associated data at any time. Once processed, this action cannot be undone.

What will be deleted

Submitting a deletion request will permanently remove:

  • Your account credentials (email / Google Sign-In link)
  • Your profile: callsign, display name, and profile photo
  • All games you created or participated in
  • Your team memberships and role assignments
  • All mission reports you submitted, including attached photos and GPS coordinates
Firebase Authentication Cloud Firestore Firebase Storage

Data is stored in Google Firebase. All records are permanently removed from Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and Storage within 30 days of your request.

How to request deletion

  1. Tap the button below to open a pre-filled email.
  2. Include the email address linked to your AirsoftOps account so we can locate and delete the correct data.
  3. Send the email — we will confirm receipt and process your request within 30 days.
Request Account Deletion

This action is irreversible. All your data — games, missions, reports, and photos — will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. If you change your mind, you would need to create a new account.

Alternative: delete from within the app

You can also delete your account directly inside AirsoftOps: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. In-app deletion is processed immediately.
