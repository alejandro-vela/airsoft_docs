AirsoftOps — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

AirsoftOps ("we", "our", or "us") is a tactical coordination application for airsoft games. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address (sign-up with email/password or Google Sign-In).
  • Google account display name and profile photo, when you use Google Sign-In.
  • Callsign (in-game username) you choose during onboarding.

Game & activity data

  • Games you create or participate in (name, field, timestamps, status).
  • Team membership and assigned role (superuser, squad leader, operator, observer).
  • Missions assigned to you and their completion status.

Mission reports (user-submitted)

  • Written notes you add when completing a mission.
  • Photos you choose to attach to a report.
  • GPS coordinates you optionally include in a report to indicate your position on the field.

What we do NOT collect

No passive location tracking No contacts No microphone No advertising ID No device fingerprinting

Location data is only stored when you explicitly submit a mission report with coordinates. The app never tracks your location in the background.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To display your profile, role, and team assignments within the app.
  • To enable the core tactical coordination features (game management, missions, reports).
  • To allow other players in the same game to see your callsign and assigned missions.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only with the following third-party service providers strictly to operate the app:

Google Firebase (Google LLC)

  • Firebase Authentication — manages your login credentials.
  • Cloud Firestore — stores your profile, game data, and mission reports.
  • Firebase Storage — stores photos you attach to reports.

Google's privacy practices: policies.google.com/privacy

Google Sign-In (Google LLC)

If you log in with Google, we receive your email, display name, and profile photo from your Google account solely for authentication purposes.

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account remains active. When you request account deletion (see Section 6), all personal information associated with your account is permanently removed from our databases within 30 days.

5. Data Security

All data is stored in Google Cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit. Access to Firestore is controlled by Firebase Security Rules so that users can only read or modify data within games they belong to. No sensitive data is stored locally on your device beyond a secure authentication token.

6. Your Rights & Account Deletion

  • Access: You can view all data associated with your account directly in the app.
  • Correction: You can update your callsign and profile at any time.
  • Deletion: To permanently delete your account and all associated data, send an email to devalejo.sa@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion – AirsoftOps". We will process your request within 30 days.
  • Data portability: You may request a copy of your data by emailing us at the address above.

7. Children's Privacy

AirsoftOps is intended for users 13 years of age or older. Airsoft is an activity that typically requires adult supervision for minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at devalejo.sa@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. For significant changes, we will notify users within the app.
